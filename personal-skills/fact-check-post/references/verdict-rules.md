# Verdict rules

- **True** — reliable evidence confirms the material parts of the claim.
- **Partly true** — evidence confirms a meaningful part, but context, scope, timing, or a number is wrong or missing.
- **False** — reliable evidence directly contradicts the material claim.
- **Insufficient evidence** — reliable evidence is unavailable or cannot establish the claim.

Use **high** confidence for direct, current primary evidence; **medium** for several reliable secondary sources or partial primary evidence; **low** when evidence is indirect, conflicting, or time-sensitive.
