# Source hierarchy

Prefer sources in this order:

1. The primary record: official statistics, law, court document, company filing, original study, or direct statement by the responsible organization.
2. A reputable independent source that links to or accurately quotes the primary record.
3. Specialist databases or research organizations with a clear method.
4. Unverified posts, screenshots, and reposts only as leads; never use them as sole proof.

Check publication date, event date, jurisdiction, definitions, and whether the cited figure covers the same group or period as the claim. For evolving events, favor the newest primary source and note when facts may change.
