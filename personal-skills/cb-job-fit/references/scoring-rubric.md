# C&B fit rubric

Score each relevant requirement from 0 to 2:

- **2** — directly demonstrated in the user's supplied experience.
- **1** — adjacent experience or a credible, short learning path.
- **0** — no evidence or a material mismatch.

Weight essential duties, scope, and required systems more than preferred skills. A role is usually a strong fit when essential requirements are mostly 2s; recommend applying with caveat when there are one or two learnable essential gaps; recommend skipping when several hard requirements score 0.

Always list the evidence that supports each material score.
