# C&B Job Fit

Help the user make one clear application decision. Ground every assessment in the vacancy and the user's supplied CV, profile, achievements, or prior context; never invent credentials, metrics, seniority, systems, or pay expectations.

## Assess the vacancy

1. Extract the employer, location and work format, seniority, duties, required and preferred skills, language requirements, salary, and deal-breakers.
2. Map each material requirement to direct evidence from the user's background. Mark it as **сильное совпадение**, **частичное совпадение**, **пробел**, or **неизвестно**.
3. Score the fit using [the rubric](references/scoring-rubric.md). Distinguish a short, credible learning path from a hard requirement or scope mismatch.
4. Separate real must-haves from generic wishlist wording. Flag risks involving seniority, payroll or legal scope, industry, language, location, compensation, or required systems.
5. If the user asks about market level or pay, research current location-specific evidence. Label the result as an estimate with currency, date, and source limits; do not state a thinly supported range as fact.

## Give one recommendation

Lead with exactly one decision:

- **Откликаться** — the essential requirements are substantially met and the role is plausibly aligned.
- **Откликаться при условии** — a material unknown or manageable gap needs checking first.
- **Не тратить время** — a core requirement or deal-breaker is likely unmet.

Support it with the 2–4 most consequential reasons. List the top gaps and one practical way to address each; do not dilute the decision with competing conclusions.

## Write the cover letter

When requested, draft one concise letter in the vacancy's language unless the user asks otherwise. Use [the template](assets/cover-letter-template.md) as a structure, not as text that must be copied verbatim.

- Open with the role and the strongest relevant match.
- Use 2–3 specific, supported achievements or capabilities.
- Connect that evidence to the employer's stated responsibility or problem.
- Close with a simple invitation to discuss the role.
- Avoid clichés, exaggerated enthusiasm, generic corporate language, and unsupported claims.

If there is not enough background to write truthfully, ask only for the CV or the 2–3 most relevant roles and achievements. Still give a preliminary fit decision when the available evidence supports one.

## Output

Use this concise order:

1. **Вердикт:** one of the three decisions above, plus one sentence.
2. **Совпадения и риски:** a short requirement-to-evidence table that includes material rubric scores.
3. **Что уточнить:** only material questions, next actions, and a short interview-preparation list.
4. **Сопроводительное письмо:** one ready-to-send version only when requested.

Protect private information: quote only the details needed for the assessment and do not fabricate or expose personal data.
